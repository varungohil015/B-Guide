# 10 Metrics

## Core Numbers To Track Weekly
### Sales pipeline
- leads generated
- emails sent
- positive replies
- calls booked
- calls completed
- proposals sent
- deals closed

### Financial
- monthly recurring revenue
- monthly profit
- average client value
- client acquisition cost if tracked

### Delivery
- number of active clients
- videos delivered
- turnaround time
- revision count
- delivery bottlenecks

### Retention
- client satisfaction
- retention length
- churn reasons
- testimonials/case studies collected
