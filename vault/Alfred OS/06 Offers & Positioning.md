# 06 Offers & Positioning

## Core Offer
A Done-For-You content engine for talking-head creators, coaches, and business/finance founders.

## Offer Promise
The client records. We handle the rest.

## Included
- Content ideation
- Topic and angle development
- Script writing / script refinement
- Hook and retention structure
- Packaging direction
- Thumbnail design
- Video editing
- Delivery workflow

## What This Is Really Selling
Do not sell editing.
Sell:
- saved founder time
- consistency
- better content operations
- speed to publish
- higher quality execution
- a repeatable content engine

## Ideal Early ICP
Best first targets are likely:
- finance creators
- coaches
- business creators with talking-head content
- founders already posting or wanting to post consistently
- people who value time more than DIY effort

## Positioning Statement Draft
We help talking-head founders turn raw recordings into a consistent content engine — covering ideation, scripts, packaging, thumbnails, and editing — so they can focus on being the expert, not the operator.

## Pricing Direction
Do not anchor the business around $1k–$2k per video as the main model.
Start testing productized monthly retainers instead.

### Suggested retainer thinking
- $2.5k–$3k/month entry point for DFY execution
- based on defined deliverables and turnaround
- offer can later expand into higher tiers once proof stack improves

## Work To Finalize
- exact monthly deliverables
- revision policy
- turnaround promises
- communication method
- onboarding flow
- guarantee or risk-reversal if any
- minimum contract length if any

## Rules
- simplicity beats cleverness
- productized beats custom
- retainer beats random one-off work
