# 02 Strategy

## Strategic Direction
Move from selling editing as a task to selling a productized Done-For-You content engine for talking-head creators in finance, coaching, and business.

## Core Strategic Shift
### Old model
- Sell editing
- Trade time for money
- Founder stays the bottleneck
- Revenue depends on personal labor

### New model
- Sell outcomes, consistency, speed, and time savings
- Productize delivery
- Delegate fulfillment
- Founder focuses on sales, positioning, systems, and growth

## Working Thesis
Best path to scale:
1. Productized DFY offer
2. Strong outbound engine
3. Proof-led inbound authority
4. Delegated fulfillment
5. Tight weekly operating rhythm

## Main Strategic Insight From Matt Gary Notes
The problem is not lack of effort. The problem is that the founder is still acting as the system.

## Real Goal
Build a business where:
- the client only records
- the team handles ideation, scripting, packaging, editing, and delivery
- ziscol spends most of his time on growth, not fulfillment

## Revenue Logic
Target monthly personal profit: $10k–$12k
Likely path:
- sell a $2.5k–$3k/month DFY package
- close 4–5 solid clients
- keep margins healthy through productized delivery and delegation

## Strategic Rules
- Do not compete on cheap editing
- Do not customize every client workflow from scratch
- Do not let founder hours be the primary growth engine
- Build systems before adding complexity
- Every repeated task should become an SOP
