# 08 Content & Brand

## Objective
Build authority without becoming a face-first creator.

## Core Brand Principle
Use proof, clarity, and useful analysis. Avoid fluffy motivation.

## Recommended Content Model
Focus on proof-led content.

### Core content types
- case studies
- before/after breakdowns
- content system explainers
- mistakes founders make with talking-head content
- packaging and retention insights
- behind-the-scenes lessons from building the agency

## Faceless Brand Advantage
ziscol does not need to become an on-camera influencer.
He can build authority through:
- text posts
- carousels
- visuals
- teardown posts
- client result breakdowns
- articles

## Weekly Content Asset From Notes
Build a generous lead magnet / conversion asset that explains the DFY system.
Purpose:
- prove competence
- create trust
- convert attention into booked calls

## Suggested First Asset
A 3–5 page document that explains:
- the content workflow
- how ideation is handled
- how scripts are structured
- how packaging is decided
- how editing is used to improve retention
- why founders should stop doing this manually
- CTA to book a call

## Suggested LinkedIn Post Framework
1. Mirror the founder's current pain
2. Show the cost of doing content manually
3. Present the system shift
4. Offer the lead magnet
5. Use a tight CTA

## Content Rule
Do not post vague advice.
Post useful proof that makes the reader think: this person actually understands the game.
