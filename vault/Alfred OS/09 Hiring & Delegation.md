# 09 Hiring & Delegation

## Current Team
- ziscol
- editor/friend
- lead generator

## Main Delegation Goal
Move execution away from ziscol so he can focus on growth.

## Immediate Team Design
### ziscol
Owns:
- offer
- sales
- outreach
- positioning
- content strategy
- review and quality control
- systems design

### editor/friend
Owns:
- editing execution
- applying style guides
- making revisions
- following SOPs

### lead generator
Owns:
- lead sourcing
- list quality
- prospect research
- helping fill outbound pipeline

## Key Operating Rule
If ziscol is still doing the core fulfillment himself, growth will stall.

## Delegation System
- Every repeated task gets recorded once
- Convert recordings into SOPs
- Store SOPs in one place
- Use a review loop to improve quality

## Reporting Rhythm
Daily end-of-day reports from each team member:
- what was completed
- blockers
- next actions

## Meeting Cadence
Three short weekly meetings can work:
- Monday: priorities and targets
- Wednesday: bottlenecks and fixes
- Friday: scoreboard and lessons
