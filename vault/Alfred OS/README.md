# Alfred OS

This folder is Alfred's working area for helping ziscol grow the company.

## Purpose

Keep track of:
- business analysis
- growth plans
- offers and positioning
- sales and outreach systems
- hiring and delegation
- content strategy
- decisions made
- next actions

## Structure

- `00 Inbox.md` — quick captures, raw notes, loose thoughts
- `01 Company Snapshot.md` — current state of the business
- `02 Strategy.md` — high-level strategy and priorities
- `03 Execution Plan.md` — current operating plan
- `04 Decisions Log.md` — important decisions and why they were made
- `05 Questions.md` — open questions that need answers
- `06 Offers & Positioning.md` — offer design and messaging
- `07 Sales & Outreach.md` — acquisition systems and sales process
- `08 Content & Brand.md` — authority building and content strategy
- `09 Hiring & Delegation.md` — team, SOPs, delegation, org design
- `10 Metrics.md` — core numbers and weekly scoreboard

This is meant to stay practical, not pretty.
