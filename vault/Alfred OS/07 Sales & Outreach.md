# 07 Sales & Outreach

## Primary Channels
- Cold email
- LinkedIn
- Referrals

## Immediate Sales Principle
Sell certainty, simplicity, and outcomes — not hours or editing effort.

## What Prospecting Needs First
- clear ICP
- clear offer
- clear proof
- clear CTA

## Outbound System
### Cold Email
Goal: start conversations with qualified creators/founders who are already publishing or clearly need a better content system.

Need:
- lead list by niche
- clean offer angle
- short direct opener
- problem insight
- low-friction CTA
- follow-up sequence

## Inbound Support
LinkedIn should support outbound by making ziscol look credible.
Needed:
- clean headline
- clear banner
- featured proof asset
- proof-led posts
- case studies and breakdowns

## Sales Call Structure
### 60-minute structure
1. Discovery
- current content output
- bottlenecks
- goals
- current team/setup
- what is costing them time or growth

2. Pitch
- explain the DFY content engine simply
- show how the process removes work from the client
- explain expected workflow and deliverables

3. Objections
- price
- timing
- trust
- partner/team approval

## Early Outreach Angle Ideas
- founder spending too much time editing
- inconsistent posting because workflow is broken
- strong expertise but weak content packaging
- no system from idea to published asset
