Mission Launch

Prerequisites

Before touching Instantly, the following must already be in place. If even one of these is missing, do not proceed.

1. A Verified Lead List (Minimum 1,000 Leads)

You must have:

● At least 1,000 leads

● Each lead manually reviewed

This is not the place to test questionable data. Outreach magnifies both quality and mistakes. 2. At Least Three Warmed-Up Inboxes

You must have:

● A minimum of three inboxes

● Purchased through Instantly DFY or pre-warmed

These inboxes must:

● Be fully warmed

● Forwarding set to your landing page domain

3. Outreach Strategy Alignment

If you are using the 3C Outreach Strategy:

● Every lead must have a personalized first line

● That personalization must exist as a separate column in your lead list If you are not using 3C:

● Personalization is optional

● You do not need a personalization column

4. Instantly Growth Plan

You must be on the Growth Plan inside Instantly.

Lower plans will restrict sending capacity and campaign controls.

5. Completed Outreach Sequence

All emails must already be written:

● Initial outreach email

● Follow-ups (4 total emails recommended)

● Subject line finalized

This module assumes copy is finalized. We are deploying, not writing.

Step 1: Logging Into the Correct Instantly Account Go to instantly.ai and log in.

Ensure you are logging into:

● The account that contains your warmed domains and inboxes

● Not a test account

● Not a backup account

Before proceeding, confirm:

● Your inboxes are visible

● Their warm-up status is active or completed

Once confirmed, move to the campaign setup.

Step 2: Creating a New Campaign

Navigate to the Campaigns tab.

Click Add New Campaign.

Campaign Naming Convention

Name the campaign in a way that allows immediate clarity later.

A strong format is:

● Audience + Date + Strategy

Step 3: Uploading Your Lead List

After creating the campaign, you’ll land on the Leads tab.

Click Add Leads.

Uploading the CSV

● Upload your lead list as a CSV file

● If your leads came from a scraper:

○ Open the file

○ Click File → Export → CSV

○ Upload that exported file

Once uploaded, Instantly will prompt you to map columns.

Step 4: Mapping Lead Variables Correctly

This step is critical. Incorrect mapping leads to broken personalization and failed sends. Required Mappings

● First Name → First Name

● Email → Email

These must be mapped correctly.

Columns You Should NOT Import

If your list contains:

● YouTube links

● Profile URLs

Personalization Mapping (3C Only)

If you are using the 3C strategy:

● Map your personalization column to a custom variable

● Typically labeled something like:

○ {{personalization}}

If you are not using 3C:

● Skip this entirely

Once mapping is complete:

● Click Next

● Import all leads

Step 5: Building the Outreach Sequence

After importing leads, navigate to the Sequence tab.

Subject Line:

Paste the subject line you finalized in the previous module.

Do not improvise here.

Step 1: Initial Outreach Email

Paste your first outreach email into Step 1.

If you need variables:

● Click the Variables button in the bottom-right corner

● Insert:

○ First name

○ Personalization (if applicable)

Previewing the Email

Before moving on:

● Click Preview

● Select a lead

● Read the email as if you were the recipient

Confirm:

● Variables populate correctly

● Formatting is clean

● No broken placeholders

Adding Follow-Ups

Click Add Step.

For each follow-up:

● Paste the corresponding email

● Repeat for:

○ Second follow-up

○ Third follow-up

○ Fourth follow-up

Do not modify timing unless you have a specific reason. Default delays are sufficient for initial launch.

Step 6: Campaign Settings (Do Not Skip)

Schedule

Leave the schedule unchanged.

The default US & Canada working hours are ideal:

● This is where the majority of prospects in this space operate

● Over-optimizing here creates unnecessary risk

Accounts to Use

Select:

● The three warmed inboxes you prepared earlier

Do not include cold or backup inboxes.

Reply Handling

Enable:

● Stop sending emails on reply

This prevents follow-ups being sent to someone who has already responded.

Tracking Settings

Disable:

● Open Tracking

Open tracking is unreliable due to:

● Apple Mail Privacy Protection

● Automatic opens that distort data

● Inconsistent deliverability signals

Tracking opens adds no meaningful insight at this stage.

Delivery Optimization

Enable:

● Send Emails as Text Only

● No HTML

Plain text emails:

● Look more human

● Deliver better

● Reduce spam signals

Daily Sending Limits

Start conservatively.

● 10 emails per inbox per day

● With three inboxes = 30 emails/day total

Scaling plan:

● Week 1: 30/day

● Week 2: 60/day

● Week 3: 90/day

This gradual ramp protects domain reputation and ensures longevity.

Advanced Options

Do not touch Advanced Options.

Default settings are sufficient and safe.

Step 7: Launching the Campaign

Once all settings are confirmed:

Click Go.

That’s it.

Your campaign is live.

Emails will begin sending according to schedule, limits, and inbox rotation.
