# Master Principles

Synthesized across all Kallaway marketing transcripts in this folder.

## Core principles

- **Ideas are the highest-leverage part of content.** Topic, premise, and hook matter more than editing polish.
- **Packaging is make-or-break.** Titles, thumbnails, hooks, and intros determine whether good content even gets a chance.
- **Pick the right game.** Business owners should usually choose category authority / buyer-building over generic creator growth.
- **Education and entertainment should be chosen intentionally.** The middle usually loses.
- **Psychology drives performance.** Desire, curiosity, clarity, novelty, and comprehension are the key levers.
- **Build an audience of buyers, not empty viewers.**
- **Strong personal brands create pull, trust, and premium economics.**
- **Visuals often communicate faster than words.**
- **Systems beat inspiration.** Reusable workflows for ideas, scripts, packaging, recording, and analysis create consistent output.
- **Human authenticity becomes more valuable as AI content rises.**
