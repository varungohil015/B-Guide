# Master Rules

## Rules repeated across the folder

1. Pick one clear content game before posting.
2. Stay out of the middle between useful and entertaining.
3. Improve ideas before worrying about editing polish.
4. Design for topic clarity and curiosity in every hook.
5. Treat packaging as part of the content, not an afterthought.
6. Build for buyers if the goal is business growth.
7. Use repeatable frameworks and named concepts.
8. Study winners and remix patterns instead of guessing from scratch.
9. Optimize for comprehension with strong visuals and simple flow.
10. Build systems that increase speed without lowering judgment.
11. Avoid the viral trap if it weakens audience quality.
12. Preserve strong human signal as AI content gets more common.
