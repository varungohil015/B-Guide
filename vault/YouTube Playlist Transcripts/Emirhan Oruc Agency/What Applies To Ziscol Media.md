# What Applies To Ziscol Media

## Immediate moves

- Build the core offer around a business result: more qualified leads, booked calls, or revenue-supporting authority.
- Pick one niche where ziscol can understand the buyer deeply and talk in their language.
- Use personalized outbound with short video audits instead of generic DMs.
- Create a simple acquisition tracker and review it weekly.
- Push early clients toward testimonial + case study + measurable before/after proof.

## Next-stage moves

- Move from “content service” to “growth system support.”
- Connect content to distribution and lead capture so results are visible.
- Start documenting SOPs from day one for research, scripting, editing, posting, and reporting.
- Design retention mechanisms: monthly strategy, reporting, new angles, and pipeline visibility.
- Build a small trusted bench for editing/admin/ops before burnout hits.

## Strategic caution

- Avoid becoming a cheap content executor.
- Avoid broad generic offers.
- Avoid scaling client acquisition before proof, offer clarity, and fulfillment quality exist.
- Avoid building a business that depends on ziscol doing every task personally.
