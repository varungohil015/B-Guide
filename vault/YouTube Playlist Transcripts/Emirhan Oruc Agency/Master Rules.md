# Master Rules

## Rules repeated across the folder

1. Do not sell “just editing” if you want premium pricing and retention.
2. Pick a niche with money, urgency, and reachable decision-makers.
3. Make the offer specific enough that outreach feels tailored, not generic.
4. Track every stage of outbound and improve the weakest KPI first.
5. Stop using cold DMs as the main growth engine if they blend in with the crowd.
6. Use proof in everything: outreach, sales calls, content, and follow-up.
7. Solve more of the client’s growth system than competitors do.
8. Keep clients longer by tying work to revenue, not vanity metrics.
9. Delegate once the workflow is known; don’t wait until burnout forces it.
10. Build repeatable systems before chasing big scale.
11. Treat every client win as future marketing material.
12. Optimize for long-term profit, not short-term hustle aesthetics.
