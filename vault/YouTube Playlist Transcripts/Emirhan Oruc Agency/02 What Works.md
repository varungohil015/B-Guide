# 02 What Works

## Recurring tactics and structures that the transcripts repeatedly endorse

### 1) A hybrid offer
What keeps showing up:
- Organic content creation
- Some form of paid amplification (often profile visit ads / boosted content)
- Appointment setting from followers, leads, or traffic
- Sometimes sales support / funnel / webinar / scripting help

Why it works:
- It turns “attention” into booked calls.
- It gives the client a clearer line to revenue.
- It lets you charge higher monthly retainers.

### 2) Niche selection with strong economics
What works best:
- Niches with high-ticket offers
- Clients with enough margin and cash to reinvest
- Decision-makers you can reach directly
- Markets you understand or can quickly understand

Why it works:
- ROI is easier to create and explain.
- Closing is easier when the upside is obvious.

### 3) Specific positioning and sub-niching
Examples implied in the transcripts:
- Not just “fitness coaches” but a narrower profitable slice
- Not just “coaches” but a very specific type with a specific buyer problem

Why it works:
- Outreach lands harder.
- Prospects feel understood.
- Sales calls get easier because the problem-solution fit is clearer.

### 4) Personalized outreach with decent volume
What works:
- Personalized Looms
- Outreach that points to a real opportunity/problem
- Doing enough volume after the message is fixed
- Multi-step follow-up

Why it works:
- Better than generic compliments or template spam.
- Good inputs + enough reps produce predictable pipeline.

### 5) Proactive monetization of existing attention
What works:
- Messaging existing followers/leads instead of waiting
- Turning audience attention into appointments through setters
- Starting monetization immediately rather than waiting weeks for new content to perform

Why it works:
- Faster cash generation
- Better use of current assets
- Useful for getting early wins and proof

### 6) Results-based marketing
What works:
- Using client outcomes and case studies as the core marketing engine
- Publishing proof so stronger clients come in
- Letting fulfillment feed acquisition

Why it works:
- Better proof creates more trust.
- Better trust creates better sales efficiency.
- Better clients then create even better proof.

### 7) Delegation of repetitive work
What works:
- Setter handles repetitive outreach/follow-up/booking work
- Editors / operators handle delivery pieces
- Founder keeps high-leverage roles longer: closing, strategy, offer decisions

Why it works:
- Frees time for marketing and growth
- Stops the founder from becoming the bottleneck
- Makes scaling beyond survival mode possible

### 8) Client care that goes beyond the base service
What works:
- Helping with sales, funnel, team, traffic, appointment flow, and scaling constraints
- Acting more like a growth partner than a narrow service vendor

Why it works:
- Clients stay longer
- Results improve
- Retention and referrals become more likely
