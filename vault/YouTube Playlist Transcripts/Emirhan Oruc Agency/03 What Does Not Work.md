# 03 What Does Not Work

## Repeated anti-patterns across the transcripts

### 1) Selling a vague service with no direct ROI
- “We make content” by itself is repeatedly framed as weak.
- It leads to low prices, low conviction, bad retention, and difficult scaling.

### 2) Staying too generic
- Broad niches and broad messaging make outreach harder and positioning weaker.
- If everyone can say the same thing, you become replaceable.

### 3) Doing more of broken outreach
- Sending huge volume with a weak angle is a trap.
- One transcript explicitly contrasts 400 looms with zero booked calls versus far better results after correcting the approach.

### 4) Waiting for prospects to come to you
- Getting followers/traffic but not having setters proactively book appointments is framed as wasted opportunity.

### 5) Founder doing everything
- Recording looms, setting, follow-ups, taking calls, closing, running fulfillment, managing team, and building assets all at once = plateau.
- This kills time for marketing and strategic work.

### 6) Reactive fulfillment
- Constantly solving random client complaints instead of running a standardized results system creates chaos.
- It also makes scale fragile.

### 7) Relying only on referrals or inconsistent inbound
- The transcripts push toward a predictable acquisition machine, not hoping word of mouth saves the month.

### 8) Low-quality clients and bad-fit clients
- Cheap, demanding, or structurally poor-fit clients create drama, weak results, and low freedom.
- Keeping them can block the next growth phase.

### 9) Optimizing the wrong things early
- Website tweaking, random busywork, and generic “inputs” are portrayed as fake progress.
- The focus should be on niche, offer, outreach, booked calls, results, proof, and systems.

### 10) Quitting or switching too early
- A major recurring lesson is that many people change direction in the “valley of despair” before they have actually given the adjusted strategy enough time.

### 11) Treating cold outreach like a magic script problem only
- Scripts matter, but the transcripts repeatedly imply the deeper issue is usually market + offer + positioning + follow-up structure.

### 12) Building a business that collapses when you step away
- Revenue that depends on the founder’s nonstop presence is treated as a warning sign, not a success state.
