# Best Ideas

## Highest-signal ideas worth stealing

- **Loom outreach as default outbound.** Personalized video proves competence faster than a text pitch.
- **KPI-driven acquisition.** Break outbound into sent, viewed, replied, booked, closed, retained.
- **Retention as compounding revenue.** The easiest path to more cash is often keeping clients longer.
- **Hybrid delivery.** Content + traffic + setter/sales support creates stronger client outcomes.
- **Fulfillment as marketing.** Great results become case studies, proof screenshots, interviews, and referrals.
- **Growth engineering.** Map client attention, trust, conversion, and sales leaks rather than offering isolated execution.
- **Idea-first content.** Strong hooks/angles matter more than production polish.
- **Systemized delegation.** Founder only owns high-leverage parts once delivery is documented.
- **Trust-based ads/funnels.** Warm content and believable proof beat hard-sell funnels.
