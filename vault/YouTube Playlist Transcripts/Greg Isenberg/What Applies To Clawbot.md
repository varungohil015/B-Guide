# What Applies To Clawbot

## Best direct applications

### 1) Morning brief / heartbeat systems
- Weather, calendar, email priorities, project status, trends, competitor updates.
- Low drama, high daily value.

### 2) Research and intelligence agent
- Track trends on X/Reddit/web.
- Summarize competitor activity.
- Surface product and content opportunities.

### 3) Content support system
- Repurpose content
- generate ideas
- summarize sources
- maintain content pipeline
- review analytics and suggest next experiments

### 4) CRM / follow-up support
- Draft follow-ups
- summarize lead context
- suggest who to contact next
- update CRM with human review

### 5) Workflow self-improvement
- Build dashboards, trackers, checklists, and skills for itself.
- Clawbot improving Clawbot workflow is a recurring high-value pattern.

### 6) Obsidian-backed memory and process
- Keep style guides, templates, workflows, user context, and outputs in files.
- Makes Clawbot more reusable and less stateless.

## Best implementation principles
- Use separate threads/workspaces/personas for separate jobs.
- Keep permissions narrow.
- Use PRs/drafts instead of auto-publish.
- Create reusable skill-like prompts/process docs for recurring tasks.
- Tie work to measurable outcomes where possible.

## Good Clawbot opportunities for ziscol
- founder brief
- trend scout
- content repurposer
- directory/research assistant
- CRM follow-up copilot
- market/opportunity analyst

## Things to avoid or heavily supervise
- Full social account autonomy
- broad email autonomy with no filters
- finance/trading execution
- prod deploys without approval
- any public action where prompt injection or account abuse would be costly
