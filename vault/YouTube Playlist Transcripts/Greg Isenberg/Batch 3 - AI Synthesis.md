## Best AI use cases from this batch

### 1) AutoResearch / optimize loops
- Core idea: define a measurable goal and let AI run repeated experiments, keep winners, discard losers.
- Beyond ML research, this generalizes to:
  - landing page optimization
  - ad creative testing
  - pricing tests
  - lead qualification rules
  - internal workflow optimization
  - research/due diligence production
- This is a powerful pattern for “agentic improvement”, not just content generation.

### 2) OpenClaw for revenue-generating ops
- Clear business angle: use OpenClaw/computer-use agents for paid automation work.
- Good entry route: mine Upwork for real pain already being paid for.
- Better route: specialize by vertical and turn one-off automations into reusable workflow assets.

### 3) AI-native SaaS playbook
- Strong overall framework from Greg:
  - choose a subniche in a big market
  - map workflow end-to-end
  - find where money changes hands
  - identify repetitive mechanical steps
  - manually perform it first
  - turn mechanics into agent workflows
  - connect to real tools
  - move from seat-based pricing to per-task / outcome pricing
- This is one of the highest-signal strategic frameworks in the folder.

### 4) Research accelerators like Last30Days
- Good pattern: use live/trending social/web data to improve prompts, plans, research quality, and idea selection.
- Not necessarily a standalone business on its own unless paired with a workflow people already need.
- Strong utility layer for:
  - prompt generation
  - competitive research
  - trend scouting
  - angle finding for content and product

### 5) AI-enhanced content creation stack
- Manus + Freepik + Nano Banana + video models + editing software can materially improve creative quality.
- High-leverage use cases:
  - creator style analysis
  - converting scripts to storyboards/templates
  - improving backgrounds / scenes cheaply
  - creating strong first 3 seconds / transitions
  - maintaining a style guide in Obsidian for repeatability
- Important lesson: AI is most useful when attached to a repeatable system and style guide.

## Patterns that work
- Optimization loops need a metric. No metric = no real loop.
- Start with a real paid pain, not a cool demo.
- Manual-first before automation gives the best workflow understanding.
- Content systems work best when style is codified (templates, style guides, storyboards).
- Use trend/context tools to prime better prompts before building.
- Specialized workflow assets become the real moat.

## What does not work / pitfalls
- Optimizing without a clear business KPI.
- Overfocusing on views instead of conversions.
- Treating AI-generated aesthetics as enough without narrative structure.
- Generic “AI agency” positioning with no vertical or workflow specificity.
- Starting with a full product before proving the workflow manually.

## Practical rules
- Pick subniches, not broad markets.
- Build around repetitive mechanics and measurable outcomes.
- Charge for task/outcome, not just seats, when possible.
- Use AI for research, planning, and creative acceleration — but keep taste and final judgment human.
- Document style/process in files so AI can reuse them.
- If you can find people already paying on Upwork for the workflow, demand is real enough to test.

  - trend scouting
  - competitive analysis
  - workflow automation setup
  - prompt generation from recent market chatter
