# Master Rules

## Rules repeated across the folder

1. Do not chase views at the expense of buyer relevance.
2. Start with a good high-ticket offer and clear economics.
3. Define the ICP before ideation.
4. Build videos around pains, desires, objections, and FAQs.
5. Let the creator’s competitive edge show in the content.
6. Use titles and thumbnails that talk to the buyer directly.
7. Treat each video like a sales asset, not just content.
8. Build a clear next step: VSSL, free training, application, call, or offer page.
9. Publish for bingeability and long-term trust.
10. Favor micro-niche authority over broad creator-mode content.
11. Keep the funnel simple and repeatable.
12. Measure cash and qualified leads, not just subscribers.
