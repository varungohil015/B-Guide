# Master Principles

Synthesized across all Nate transcripts in this folder.

## Core principles

- **YouTube is a trust and sales platform first.** Searchability, watch time, and binge behavior make it stronger than short-form for high-ticket conversion.
- **Low views can still be elite economics.** For a niche buyer channel, a few hundred or few thousand right viewers is enough.
- **ICP drives everything.** Video ideas should come from pains, desires, objections, FAQs, and market sophistication.
- **Packaging matters.** Titles, thumbnails, and hooks must speak directly to the buyer.
- **Competitive edge must be visible.** Story, proof, personality, niche insight, and positioning should bleed through the content.
- **Think in funnels, not uploads.** Top, middle, and bottom funnel content should move viewers toward a call, application, or purchase.
- **VSSL / free training / my story videos are core conversion assets.**
- **A strong offer is non-negotiable.** YouTube amplifies the offer; it does not rescue a weak one.
- **Evergreen content compounds.** Good videos keep generating leads and sales long after posting.
- **Narrow authority beats broad virality for business channels.**
