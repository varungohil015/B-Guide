# Best Ideas

## Highest-signal ideas worth stealing

- **YouTube as long-form nurture.** Use it to shorten the sales cycle and pre-sell before calls.
- **Small audience, big cash math.** High-ticket + right audience > huge reach.
- **TOF / MOF / BOF video architecture.**
- **YouTube VSSLs.** My-story and free-course style videos that act as scalable closers.
- **ICP-first ideation.** Reverse pains, objections, desires, and FAQs into topics.
- **Competitive-edge positioning.** Let proof, story, humor, or insight create differentiation.
- **Searchable evergreen trust assets.** One video can keep paying for months.
- **Micro-niche channels.** Build niche authority instead of broad creator appeal.
- **Content ecosystem thinking.** Video -> lead magnet / training -> application -> sale.
