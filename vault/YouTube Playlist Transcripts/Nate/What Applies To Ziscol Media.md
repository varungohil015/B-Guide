# What Applies To Ziscol Media

## Immediate moves

- If YouTube is used, build it for buyers in one niche, not for broad creator growth.
- Create a simple content funnel: attraction videos, nurture/proof videos, conversion videos.
- Turn sales objections and client questions into the first wave of content.
- Add one clear CTA path from every video.

## Strong opportunities

- Use YouTube as the main authority layer supporting outbound, referrals, and future paid traffic.
- Create a flagship VSL-style authority video once the offer and niche are clear.
- Package around real outcomes and specific pains rather than “marketing tips.”
- Build a library of evergreen videos that can keep warming prospects while ziscol works elsewhere.

## Strategic caution

- Don’t chase subscriber count as the primary KPI.
- Don’t make entertainment-style content unless that is truly the business model.
- Don’t overcomplicate the funnel early; simple but intentional is enough.
